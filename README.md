# Retail Sales Performance Analysis – Microsoft Excel 

## Executive Summary 
JPT Retail Stores Ltd achieved total revenue of $1,787,419.92, of which profit was $453,870.44 from 1,825 
transactions, with a good and consistent gross margin of roughly 25.4% in the fiscal year 2023. Product level 
results were consistent across 5 categories of Products (Books, Electronics, Clothing, Home Goods, Sports) with 
no major seasonal peaks and troughs during the year. The obvious area that requires management focus is the 
Eastern sales region which was approximately 16% under the best performing region, while also needing to 
improve the data quality within their area (by removing zero-value sales entries, and correctly labelling product 
categories) in order to maintain the accuracy of reporting. 
## Business Problem
Finding Items with poor sale value, Organizing data provided in the dataset.

## Objective

This report presents an analysis of JPT Retail Stores Ltd.'s retail sales data, conducted as a Week 1 portfolio 
project for the Professional Certificate in Data Analysis. As Junior Data Analyst, I was asked by the Sales 
Director to summarize company sales performance and provide evidence-based insights to support management 
decision-making. The dataset covers 1,821 sales transactions recorded across the 2023 financial year, spanning 
five product categories (Electronics, Clothing, Home Goods, Sports, and Books) and four sales regions (North, 
South, East, West). 

## Dataset
Sales Dataset of various household goods.

## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel

## Analysis

Four are offered to management; they include balancing investments across all 5 product segments, undertaking a 
quality of data audit before the next reporting period, targeting Book and Electronic segments via aggressive 
advertising, and probing sales deficit in the Eastern region. 

## Key Findings
Notable Findings are: No one product category is outperforming sales. It appears that I have healthy, diversified 
product. Sales have performed the highest in both West and South and performed low in East which is behind roughly by $77,000. Monthly sales have relatively stable with sales on Jul has been highest and lowest in Feb and 
there exist data entry issues which lead to recording 45 records with value of $0 and 7 record with incorrect 
category, under reporting total sales performance. 
 
## Recommendations

Maintain balanced investment across all five product categories. 
Prioritize a data quality audit before the next reporting cycle. 
Promote Books and Electronics through targeted marketing.
